# Completion and Evidence

Completion is a gate, not a tone of voice. A feature orchestration may be marked complete only when the requested scope, applicable rules, changed files, deterministic verification, and remaining uncertainty have been compared.

The final receipt must state:

- what changed and why;
- which user decisions shaped the result;
- what was verified and how;
- what was not verified;
- remaining risks or follow-up work.

If verification fails, return to the responsible phase. If the result differs from the approved scope, stop and ask whether to revise the scope or the implementation. Never hide an unexecuted test, unresolved conflict, or unverified assumption behind a successful-sounding summary.
